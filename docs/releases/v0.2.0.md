Daily iPhone use: LanShare now copes with the things that happen every day on a phone. It reconnects by itself, keeps what you type while the computer is away, and does not lose an upload when the screen would lock or the Mac would sleep. Tested on a real iPhone 16 Pro Max (iOS 27.2) and a MacBook Pro (macOS 27.2).

## What's new

- **Reconnects by itself**: the Home Screen app opens and connects without a tap when Wi-Fi comes back, even with cellular data on, and even when the computer was unreachable when it opened. It then shows LanShare's own **连接不上电脑** (Can't reach the computer) screen instead of Safari's error page, and keeps retrying.
- **Send while disconnected**: texts show **等待连接…** (Waiting for connection…) and arrive exactly once when the connection is back. Drafts survive switching tabs and force-quitting the app. Both stay only on your iPhone, and are deleted when you unpair.
- **Delete a single message**, on the phone or on the computer; it disappears on both sides. For a file message, the file is kept unless you choose to move it to the Trash / Recycle Bin.
- **Uploads**:
  - The iPhone screen stays on while files upload.
  - The computer does not go to sleep while files go either way.
  - A failed upload says why.
  - After the connection comes back, uploads retry by themselves, a few times at most.
  - Uploads always leave free space on the computer: 5% of the disk, at least 2 GB and at most 20 GB. An upload that does not fit is refused at once, with the real numbers.
  - A file over 4 GB is refused on a FAT32 USB stick.
- **Files page**: sort by name, modified time or size, and filter by name.
- **Launch at login** (Mac): Settings › **启动** (Startup) can start LanShare when you log in, hidden in the menu bar. It is off by default, and suggested once after your first pairing.
- **Shared folder on an external disk**: when that disk is not connected, the iPhone and Settings say so, instead of reporting a permission problem. LanShare goes back to the folder by itself once the disk is reconnected.
- **iPhone layout**: the tab bar hides while the keyboard is up, and no longer goes missing after you switch back from another app.

The interface is in Simplified Chinese. The [README](https://github.com/DEFY-AN94/LanShare#getting-started) explains how to get started.

## Upgrading from 0.1.0

- Replace the app. Your paired devices, chat history, settings and certificate are kept, and the database is upgraded on first start.
- On the iPhone, open LanShare once while it can reach the computer. It then keeps a copy of itself and opens even when the computer is away.

## Before you install

- **Neither build is signed with a developer certificate** (the Mac app has only an ad-hoc signature).
  - Mac: macOS blocks the first launch. Allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › **Open Anyway**. When macOS asks whether LanShare may accept incoming network connections, click **Allow**, or the iPhone cannot connect. It may ask again after an update.
  - Windows: SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info › Run anyway**. The installer needs administrator rights and adds a firewall rule that allows LanShare only on Private networks and only from the local subnet.
- **The Windows version has been tested only by CI**: unit and API tests, plus an automated silent install, firewall-rule check, start and uninstall. It has not been tested by hand yet. Those checks are listed under "Windows testing, later" in PLAN.md.
- The Mac build is for Apple silicon (arm64) only.
